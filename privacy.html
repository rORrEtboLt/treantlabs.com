Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how Treant Private Limited (“Treant”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data when you use our mobile apps, games, software tools and this website (together, the “Services”). It applies to every Service that links to this page, unless that Service has its own privacy policy.

1. Who we are

Treant Private Limited is a company incorporated in India (CIN U62011KA2026PTC222995), with its registered office at Beeralingeshwara Nilaya, Gavanahalli, Srinivasanagara, Rampura Post, Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka 577133. We are the data fiduciary (data controller) for personal data processed through our Services.

2. Data we collect

We try to collect as little as we need. Depending on the Service, this may include:

  • Information you give us: such as your name and email address when you contact us, sign up for an account, or send feedback.
  • App and device information: such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and anonymous identifiers, used to run the Service and fix problems.
  • Usage and diagnostics: such as crash reports, performance data and general feature usage, which help us improve stability and design.
  • Purchases: if you buy something in one of our apps, the purchase is handled by the app store (for example Apple App Store or Google Play). We receive confirmation of the purchase but not your full payment card details.
  • Content you create: data you enter in an app (such as settings, game progress or records) is normally stored on your device. If a Service syncs or backs up this data, it says so in the app.

We do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data unless a feature clearly needs it, and we will ask for your permission through your device first. You can withdraw that permission at any time in your device settings.

3. How we use data

  • To provide, maintain and secure the Services.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance and features.
  • To process purchases and restore them on your devices.
  • To reply to your messages and provide support.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Third-party services

Some Services use trusted third-party providers to operate, for example app stores, cloud hosting, crash reporting, analytics or, in free apps, advertising. These providers process data on our behalf or under their own privacy policies. Where a Service uses advertising or analytics, the relevant providers are named in that app’s store listing or in-app settings, and you can limit ad tracking through your device settings.

5. Sharing

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the Services, when required by law or to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger, with notice to you.

6. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and for a reasonable period afterwards. Diagnostic data is kept in aggregated or de-identified form where possible.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires it.

8. Children

Our Services are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) unless a Service clearly states otherwise. In that case we follow the applicable rules for children’s data, including verifiable parental consent where required, and we do not show behavioural advertising to children. If you believe a child has given us personal data without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct, update or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to exercise your rights. If an app has an account, you can request account deletion from within the app or by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the period required by law.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside India. Where this happens, we take steps to make sure your data stays protected and that the transfer is allowed by applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. For significant changes we will give notice in the relevant Service.
